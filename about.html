About Us

Pointers Insurance Brokers

Pointers Insurance Brokers Private Limited is an authorised and licensed insurance broking company that has been granted the Certificate of Registration by The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India to act as a Direct Broker (Life & General) under the IRDAI (Insurance Brokers) Regulations, 2018.

Insurance is a tool that provides protection against financial losses by transferring the risk to the Insurance Company.

We at, Pointers Insurance Brokers Private Limited, strive to make insurance easy to understand, compare, and select, helping our customers safeguard their financial interests.

Insurance is a term that comprises both product and service, and we help you find a “product” that meets your requirements for complete safety and are committed to delivering a “service” that provides immense satisfaction at the time of need.

Insurance is an intangible product that cannot be physically touched, possessed, or consumed. It is an essential service that provides peace of mind, which you experience at the time of need. Here, we offer you the confidence of relying on our expertise to choose the correct policy and our assurance to stand by you when you need us most.
