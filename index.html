    Become PoSP, Become Growth Partner..!

    PoSP stands for Point of Sales Person. It is a business model where individuals can become authorized agents for selling insurance products. As a PoSP, you can sell insurance policies to customers, receive commissions, and be part of the growing insurance ecosystem.

    Anyone who is at least 18 years old and with the minimum qualification of class 10th can become a PoSP. This role is ideal for individuals like students, housewives, retirees, or professionals looking for full/Part-time career in fastest growing insurance industry.
